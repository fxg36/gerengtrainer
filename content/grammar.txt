# do-does|Fragen mit do und does|Bei he, she und it steht does. Das Vollverb bleibt in der Grundform.
___ your neighbor work from home?|Does|Do|Doing
___ these shoes fit you?|Do|Does|Doing
Where ___ your sister live?|does|do|doing
What time ___ the shops close?|do|does|doing
___ this key open the garage?|Does|Do|Doing
# did-questions|Fragen in der Vergangenheit|Nach did steht das Vollverb in der Grundform, auch bei unregelmäßigen Verben.
Did you ___ the invoice yesterday?|send|sent|sending
Where did they ___ the ladder?|put|putting|puts
Did she ___ the train on Monday?|catch|caught|catches
When did you ___ the leak?|notice|noticed|noticing
Did he ___ the broken hinge?|replace|replaced|replaces
# present-third|He, she, it: das Verb-s|In der einfachen Gegenwart erhält das Verb bei he, she und it normalerweise die Endung -s oder -es.
My brother ___ to work every morning.|drives|drive|driving
The train ___ here every hour.|stops|stop|stopping
This recipe ___ four people.|serves|serve|serving
She ___ her emails before breakfast.|checks|check|checking
Our landlord ___ next door.|lives|live|living
# present-continuous|Was gerade passiert|Die Verlaufsform besteht aus am, is oder are und der -ing-Form.
Look! The water ___ through the ceiling.|is dripping|are dripping|is drip
Be quiet. The baby ___ right now.|is sleeping|are sleeping|is sleep
I ___ dinner at the moment.|am cooking|is cooking|am cook
They ___ the kitchen right now.|are painting|is painting|are paint
We ___ for the delayed train.|are waiting|is waiting|are wait
# irregular-past|Unregelmäßige Vergangenheit|Unregelmäßige Verben haben eigene Vergangenheitsformen. Diese Aufgabe beschreibt ein abgeschlossenes Ereignis.
Yesterday I ___ a new suitcase.|bought|buyed|buy
She ___ the last train home yesterday.|took|taked|take
We ___ the receipt in the drawer last night.|found|finded|find
He ___ his ankle during yesterday's hike.|hurt|hurted|hurting
They ___ us a refund last week.|gave|gived|give
# perfect-experience|Erfahrungen mit have und has|Das Present perfect wird mit have oder has und dem Partizip gebildet.
Have you ever ___ a deer in the wild?|seen|saw|see
She has never ___ abroad.|been|was|be
We have already ___ the deposit.|paid|pay|paying
I have just ___ the last email.|written|wrote|write
They have never ___ their own bread.|baked|bake|baking
# for-since|For und since|For bezeichnet eine Dauer; since nennt den Zeitpunkt, an dem etwas begonnen hat.
I have lived here ___ 2020.|since|for|during
We have been waiting ___ two hours.|for|since|during
She has worked here ___ last September.|since|for|during
He has owned this car ___ ten years.|for|since|during
It has been raining ___ six o'clock.|since|for|during
# past-perfect|Was vorher passiert war|Das Past perfect besteht aus had und dem Partizip. Es beschreibt ein Ereignis vor einem anderen vergangenen Ereignis.
When we arrived, the train had already ___.|left|leave|leaving
She had ___ the invoice before I called.|sent|send|sending
I realized I had ___ my keys at home.|forgotten|forgot|forget
He had never ___ a car before that lesson.|driven|drove|drive
The guests had ___ before the storm began.|arrived|arrive|arriving
# used-to|Frühere Gewohnheiten|Used to mit der Grundform beschreibt frühere Gewohnheiten oder Zustände, die heute nicht mehr gelten.
I used to ___ near the coast, but now I live inland.|live|lived|living
She used to ___ to work, but now she takes the bus.|drive|drove|driving
We used to ___ every weekend when we were students.|hike|hiked|hiking
He used to ___ glasses before his eye surgery.|wear|wore|wearing
They used to ___ a small bakery before they retired.|own|owned|owning
# going-to|Pläne mit going to|Nach am/is/are going to folgt die Grundform des Verbs.
We are going to ___ the kitchen next month.|renovate|renovated|renovating
She is going to ___ a table for tonight.|book|booked|booking
I am going to ___ the supplier tomorrow.|call|called|calling
They are going to ___ the old furniture.|sell|sold|selling
He is going to ___ a new job in November.|start|started|starting
# first-conditional|Reale Bedingungen|In dieser Form des First conditional steht nach if das Present simple; im Hauptsatz steht will mit der Grundform.
If it ___ tomorrow, we will stay indoors.|rains|will rain|raining
If she ___ now, she will catch the train.|leaves|will leave|leaving
If the shop ___ early, we will go tomorrow.|closes|will close|closing
If he ___ the receipt, he will get a refund.|brings|will bring|bringing
If this key ___, we will call the landlord.|breaks|will break|breaking
# second-conditional|Gedachte Situationen|Bei einer gedachten oder unwahrscheinlichen Situation steht hier im if-Satz eine Vergangenheitsform und im Hauptsatz would mit der Grundform.
If I had more time, I would ___ more often.|cook|cooked|cooking
If she lived closer, we would ___ her every week.|visit|visited|visiting
If we owned a car, we would ___ to the coast.|drive|drove|driving
If he knew the answer, he would ___ us.|tell|told|telling
If they had a garden, they would ___ vegetables.|grow|grew|growing
# third-conditional|Vergangene Möglichkeiten|Would have mit Partizip beschreibt hier ein vorgestelltes Ergebnis in der Vergangenheit.
If I had seen the sign, I would have ___.|stopped|stop|stopping
If she had left earlier, she would have ___ the train.|caught|catch|catching
If we had booked ahead, we would have ___ less.|paid|pay|paying
If he had worn gloves, he would have ___ his hands.|protected|protect|protecting
If they had called, I would have ___ them.|helped|help|helping
# modal-base|Nach Modalverben: Grundform|Nach can, could, should, must und might steht das Vollverb ohne to in der Grundform.
You should ___ the receipt.|keep|to keep|keeping
Can you ___ this jar?|open|to open|opening
We must ___ before noon.|leave|to leave|leaving
She might ___ a little later.|arrive|to arrive|arriving
Could you ___ me the ladder?|lend|to lend|lending
# should-have|Rückblick mit should have|Should have mit Partizip drückt aus, was rückblickend besser gewesen wäre.
I should have ___ the weather forecast.|checked|check|checking
You should have ___ me about the delay.|told|tell|telling
We should have ___ the tickets earlier.|bought|buy|buying
She should have ___ gloves for the repair.|worn|wear|wearing
He should have ___ the instructions first.|read|reading|reads
# indirect-question|Indirekte Fragen|In indirekten Fragen folgt auf das Fragewort die normale Satzstellung: Subjekt vor Verb.
Could you tell me where ___?|the station is|is the station|does the station be
Do you know when ___?|the shop opens|opens the shop|does the shop open
Can you explain why ___?|the invoice is higher|is the invoice higher|does the invoice higher
Do you remember where ___?|we parked|did we park|parked we
Could you tell me how much ___?|this costs|does this cost|costs this
# object-pronouns|Objektpronomen|Nach diesen Verben oder Präpositionen steht die Objektform: me, him, her, us oder them.
Please send the invoice to ___. (ich)|me|I|my
I saw ___ at the station. (er)|him|he|his
The landlord called ___ yesterday. (wir)|us|we|our
Could you help ___ with the luggage? (sie, Plural)|them|they|their
I brought a gift for ___. (sie, Singular)|her|she|hers
# possessives|Besitz ohne folgendes Nomen|Wenn das Nomen nicht erneut genannt wird, stehen eigenständige Possessivpronomen wie mine, yours, hers oder ours.
This is my suitcase. It is ___.|mine|my|me
Those are your gloves. They are ___.|yours|your|you
That is her coat. It is ___.|hers|her|she
This is our apartment. It is ___.|ours|our|us
Those are their tickets. They are ___.|theirs|their|them
# reflexive|Myself, yourself und weitere Formen|Reflexivpronomen beziehen sich auf das Subjekt. Hier stimmen Person und Zahl mit dem Subjekt überein.
I cut ___ while chopping onions.|myself|yourself|themselves
She taught ___ how to repair the bicycle.|herself|himself|itself
We introduced ___ to the neighbors.|ourselves|myself|yourselves
He hurt ___ while moving the cupboard.|himself|herself|themselves
Please help ___ to some cake, Anna.|yourself|myself|ourselves
# a-an|A oder an nach dem Laut|An steht vor einem Vokallaut, a vor einem Konsonantenlaut. Entscheidend ist die Aussprache.
We need ___ umbrella.|an|a|any
She is ___ honest person.|an|a|any
He works at ___ university.|a|an|any
I have ___ appointment at ten.|an|a|any
They bought ___ used car.|a|an|any
# much-many|Much und many|Many steht bei zählbaren Substantiven im Plural. Much steht bei unzählbaren Substantiven.
How ___ luggage can I take?|much|many|a few
How ___ chairs do we need?|many|much|a little
There isn't ___ milk left.|much|many|a few
How ___ screws are missing?|many|much|a little
How ___ advice did she give you?|much|many|a few
# few-little|A few und a little|A few steht bei zählbaren Dingen im Plural. A little steht bei unzählbaren Mengen.
We have ___ eggs left.|a few|a little|much
Add ___ flour to the bowl.|a little|a few|many
I need ___ information about the booking.|a little|a few|many
There are ___ towels in the cupboard.|a few|a little|much
We have ___ time before departure.|a little|a few|many
# some-any|Any in verneinten Sätzen|In diesen verneinten Sätzen wird any für eine unbestimmte Menge verwendet.
We don't have ___ spare batteries.|any|a|an
There isn't ___ furniture in the room.|any|a|an
She hasn't received ___ emails today.|any|a|an
I don't need ___ sugar in my tea.|any|a|an
They haven't made ___ reservations yet.|any|a|an
# enjoy-ing|Enjoy mit -ing|Wenn nach enjoy eine Tätigkeit folgt, steht das nächste Verb in der -ing-Form.
I enjoy ___ in the forest.|walking|to walk|walk
She enjoys ___ for friends.|cooking|to cook|cook
We enjoy ___ new places.|exploring|to explore|explore
He enjoys ___ old furniture.|restoring|to restore|restore
They enjoy ___ languages.|learning|to learn|learn
# want-infinitive|Want mit to|Nach want folgt für eine gewünschte Tätigkeit to mit der Grundform.
I want ___ the reservation.|to cancel|canceling|cancel
She wants ___ a new job.|to find|finding|find
We want ___ the apartment.|to rent|renting|rent
He wants ___ how to bake bread.|to learn|learning|learn
They want ___ before the traffic starts.|to leave|leaving|leave
# verb-preposition|Feste Präpositionen|Manche Verben werden in einer bestimmten Bedeutung mit einer festen Präposition verbunden: depend on, listen to, wait for, belong to und apologize for.
Our plans depend ___ the weather.|on|at|to
Please listen ___ the announcement.|to|at|on
We are waiting ___ the bus.|for|at|of
This suitcase belongs ___ my sister.|to|on|for
I apologize ___ the delay.|for|at|to
# relative-who|Relativsätze über Personen|Who bezieht sich in diesen Relativsätzen auf eine Person und übernimmt die Rolle des Subjekts.
The woman ___ lives next door is a doctor.|who|which|where
I know a mechanic ___ can fix that.|who|which|where
The colleague ___ sent the email is away.|who|which|where
The guest ___ booked the room has arrived.|who|which|where
The man ___ found my keys called me.|who|which|where
# comparative|Vergleiche mit than|Nach einem Komparativ steht beim direkten Vergleich than.
This suitcase is lighter ___ mine.|than|then|as
The new route is shorter ___ the old one.|than|then|as
Her apartment is bigger ___ ours.|than|then|as
The train is faster ___ the bus.|than|then|as
This repair is more expensive ___ expected.|than|then|as
# passive|Passiv mit Partizip|Das Passiv besteht aus einer Form von be und dem Partizip des Vollverbs.
The room is ___ every morning.|cleaned|clean|cleaning
The invoice was ___ yesterday.|sent|send|sending
These chairs are ___ from recycled wood.|made|make|making
The window was ___ during the storm.|broken|broke|breaking
Breakfast is ___ until ten.|served|serve|serving
# too-enough|Enough nach dem Adjektiv|Enough steht nach dem Adjektiv, wenn es ausdrückt, dass eine Eigenschaft in ausreichendem Maß vorhanden ist.
This ladder is ___ to reach the shelf.|long enough|enough long|long too
The room is ___ for four people.|large enough|enough large|large too
The soup is ___ to eat now.|cool enough|enough cool|cool too
These shoes are ___ for hiking.|comfortable enough|enough comfortable|comfortable too
The instructions are ___ to follow.|clear enough|enough clear|clear too
